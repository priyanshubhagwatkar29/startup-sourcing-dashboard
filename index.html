Scoring model
The original dataset says no rubric was supplied, so a default model was used.
100 points

Fit score and mandatory eligibility status are separate. A high score does not override a failed or unknown mandatory criterion. The score breakdowns are from the provided JSON and should be recalibrated if you adopt an agreed sourcing rubric.

Eligibility status

Pass Every applicable published criterion is supported by evidence. For Seed Fund and E-Commerce Ecosystem Builders, “Pass” means descriptive fit because the source had no hard thresholds.

Unknown Missing evidence

At least one published criterion cannot be confirmed. Treat unknown as a diligence task, not as implicit approval.

Fail Contradiction

A published criterion is contradicted by evidence. The company should not be treated as eligible for that program without a material change or correction to the record.

Inference discipline

Every financing need in the file is inferred. Do not tell a founder or investment committee that the company has requested financing unless a source confirms it.

Source quality

Prefer company announcements, official websites, filings and direct program pages. Secondary coverage can be useful, but conflicts and unverified claims remain visible.

Snapshot scope

Prepared for India and UAE scout sourcing. The supplied data was last verified on 9 October 2026; company facts, funding status and LvlUp terms may have changed.
